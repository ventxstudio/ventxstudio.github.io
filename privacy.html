Rot Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Rot Pop, a game by Ventx Studio ("we") for Android and iOS.

In short:

  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts.
  • The game has no analytics or crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob shows the ads. Google collects some device data to do that, and the game asks for your consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Data the game keeps on your phone

Rot Pop saves your level progress and scores, your lives, gems and boosters, your settings (sound, music, vibration) and a record of what you bought, so nothing is delivered twice. This data stays on your phone. It is never sent to us, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Rot Pop shows ads between levels and offers optional video ads you can watch for rewards. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game collects:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID (on iPhone, the advertising ID only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version,
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps, and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google may use this data to show ads based on your interests only where you have allowed it. Google is responsible for how it handles this data and may process it outside your country. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: on first launch, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: iOS asks whether Rot Pop may track you. If you say no, Google does not get your advertising ID. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads turns off the ads between levels. Reward videos stay available if you want them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. The game only receives a confirmation of what you bought, so it can deliver the item. We never see your card or payment details.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some US state laws, such as California's, letting ad partners use data to show interest-based ads can count as "selling" or "sharing" it. You can opt out with your device's ad settings described above and, where the game offers it, with Privacy choices in Settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, or to withdraw consent. We do not hold any personal data about you, so for ad data please use the choices above or contact Google. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Rot Pop is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
